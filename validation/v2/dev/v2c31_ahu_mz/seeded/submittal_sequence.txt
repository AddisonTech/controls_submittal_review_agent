CONTRACTOR SUBMITTAL: SEQUENCE OF OPERATION (synthetic test document, case v2c31_ahu_mz)
SUBMITTAL FOR: SECTION 23 09 93, Multizone VAV Air Handling Unit AHU-7
Paragraph numbers follow the specification. Paragraphs with a letter suffix are contractor additions.

SECTION: 3.1 | Supply Fan Control
3.1.1 The supply fan runs in the Occupied, Cool-down and Setup modes.
3.1.2 Since no zone on AHU-7 has reheat, the supply fan stays off in the Setback and Warmup modes.
3.1.3 Trim and respond logic resets the duct static pressure setpoint between a minimum of 37 Pa and a maximum of 299 Pa, with an initial value of 249 Pa.
3.1.4 At 2 minute intervals the logic lowers the setpoint by 12 Pa if there are fewer than 2 zone pressure requests, and otherwise raises it by 15 Pa per request beyond 2, limited to 37 Pa per interval.
3.1.5 With the supply fan proven on, a PI loop modulates fan speed to hold duct static pressure at setpoint.
3.1.6 The minimum supply fan speed is 20 percent.
3.1.7 The return fan is started after the supply fan proves on and is stopped whenever the supply fan stops.

SECTION: 3.2 | Supply Air Temperature Control
3.2.1 The supply air temperature loop is enabled only while the supply fan is proven on. When it is disabled, heating is off and the economizer is at minimum position.
3.2.2 In the Occupied and Setup modes the supply air temperature setpoint is 12.8 °C with outdoor air at 21.1 °C or warmer and rises linearly to the maximum setpoint with outdoor air at 15.6 °C or cooler.
3.2.3 The maximum supply air temperature setpoint is reset by trim and respond logic within the range 12.8 °C to 18.3 °C.
3.2.4 In Cool-down mode the supply air temperature setpoint is fixed at 12.8 °C.
3.2.5 In Setback and Warmup modes the supply air temperature setpoint is 35.0 °C.
3.2.6 As the loop output rises it strokes the heating coil valve closed first, then modulates the outdoor and return air dampers, and finally opens the chilled water valve.

SECTION: 3.3 | Economizer and Minimum Outdoor Air
3.3.1 Economizer operation is locked out above an outdoor air temperature of 23.9 °C and released once the outdoor air temperature drops 0.6 °C below the limit.
3.3.2 Economizer operation is disabled during freeze protection stage 2 and stage 3.
3.3.3 The minimum outdoor air damper modulates to hold the measured outdoor airflow at the 1227 L/s minimum setpoint.
3.3.4 With the supply fan off, the outdoor air dampers are closed and the return air damper is open.
3.3.5 When the economizer is enabled, the economizer outdoor air and return air dampers modulate in sequence to hold supply air temperature at setpoint.

SECTION: 3.4 | Freeze Protection
3.4.1 Stage 1: when supply air temperature stays below 4.4 °C for 5 minutes, the controller sends two heating hot water plant requests, holds the outdoor air damper at minimum position and modulates the heating coil to maintain at least 5.6 °C; stage 1 ends after the supply air temperature is above 7.2 °C for 5 minutes.
3.4.2 Stage 2: when supply air temperature stays below 3.3 °C for 5 minutes, both outdoor air dampers close for 1 hour and the controller sets a Level 3 alarm stating that minimum ventilation was interrupted.
3.4.3 Stage 3: on a freeze-stat trip, or when supply air temperature is below 3.3 °C for 15 minutes or below 1.1 °C for 5 minutes, the supply and return fans stop, the outdoor air dampers close, the cooling coil valve opens fully, the chilled water pumps are energized and a Level 2 alarm reports a freeze protection shutdown.
3.4.4 After a freeze protection shutdown the unit restarts automatically once supply air temperature recovers.

SECTION: 3.5 | Safeties and Interlocks
3.5.1 The freeze-stat is hardwired in the fan starter circuits and stops the supply and return fans independent of the BAS; the BAS monitors its status.
3.5.2 The supply and return duct smoke detectors are hardwired to stop both fans and close the outdoor air dampers; the BAS sets a Level 1 alarm on detection.
3.5.3 The high static pressure safety switch, set at 1245 Pa, stops the supply fan, which then needs a manual reset to restart.
3.5.4 The supply fan start is held off until the minimum outdoor air damper end switch proves the damper open.
