CONTRACTOR SUBMITTAL: SEQUENCE OF OPERATION (synthetic test document, case v2c25_ahu_sz)
SUBMITTAL FOR: SECTION 23 09 93, Single Zone VAV Air Handling Unit RTU-7
Paragraph numbers follow the specification. Paragraphs with a letter suffix are contractor additions.

SECTION: 3.1 | Zone Setpoints and Modes
3.1.1 The zone is held between 21.1 °C heating and 23.9 °C cooling when occupied, and between 10.0 °C heating and 29.4 °C cooling when unoccupied.
3.1.2 The occupant can offset the zone setpoints by no more than 1.1 °C up or down.
3.1.3 Outside scheduled hours the unit runs only in Setback or Setup mode when needed to hold the unoccupied setpoints.

SECTION: 3.2 | Supply Fan Control
3.2.1 In the Occupied mode the supply fan runs continuously.
3.2.2 With both loops in deadband the supply fan runs at the 30 percent minimum speed.
3.2.3 As the cooling loop goes from 50 to 100 percent, supply fan speed rises from minimum to 100 percent.
3.2.4 As the heating loop goes from 50 to 100 percent, supply fan speed rises from minimum to 50 percent.

SECTION: 3.3 | Supply Air Temperature Control
3.3.1 The cooling supply air temperature setpoint moves from 21.1 °C at 0 percent cooling loop down to 12.8 °C at 50 percent cooling loop.
3.3.2 The heating supply air temperature setpoint moves from 21.1 °C at 0 percent heating loop up to 32.2 °C at 50 percent heating loop.
3.3.3 The chilled water valve is allowed to open only when outdoor air is warmer than 12.8 °C.

SECTION: 3.4 | Economizer
3.4.1 The economizer is locked out above 23.9 °C outdoor air temperature and released when outdoor air drops 0.6 °C below that limit.
3.4.2 In heating the outdoor air damper modulates above minimum to hold mixed air temperature at 12.8 °C.
3.4.3 Minimum outdoor air damper position is interpolated linearly from 25 percent at minimum fan speed to 15 percent at maximum fan speed.

SECTION: 3.5 | Safeties and Interlocks
3.5.1 The duct smoke detector is hardwired to stop the supply fan and close the outdoor air damper, and the BAS raises a Level 1 alarm on detection.
3.5.2 The freeze-stat is hardwired to stop the supply fan and must be reset by hand.
3.5.3 On a condensate overflow switch trip the chilled water valve closes and a Level 3 alarm is set.
3.5.4 The heating and chilled water valves stay closed until the supply fan is proven on.
