CONTRACTOR SUBMITTAL: SEQUENCE OF OPERATION (synthetic test document, case v2c35_fcu)
SUBMITTAL FOR: SECTION 23 09 93, Fan Coil Unit FCU-8
Paragraph numbers follow the specification. Paragraphs with a letter suffix are contractor additions.

SECTION: 3.1 | Fan Control
3.1.1 The fan runs all the time in the Occupied mode and cycles with heating or cooling demand in Setback and Setup.
3.1.2 In deadband the fan runs at 25 percent, ramping to 100 percent as the heating or cooling loop goes from 50 to 100 percent.

SECTION: 3.2 | Supply Air Temperature Control
3.2.1 Cooling resets the supply air temperature setpoint from 21.7 °C (71 °F) at 0 percent loop down to 12.2 °C (54 °F) at 50 percent loop.
3.2.2 Heating resets the supply air temperature setpoint from 21.7 °C (71 °F) at 0 percent loop up to 32.2 °C (90 °F) at 50 percent loop.
3.2.3 The controller never opens the heating and cooling valves at the same time.

SECTION: 3.3 | Zone Setpoints
3.3.1 Occupied setpoints are 21.7 °C (71 °F) heating and 23.3 °C (74 °F) cooling, and unoccupied setpoints are 15.6 °C (60 °F) heating and 35.0 °C (95 °F) cooling.

SECTION: 3.4 | Alarms and Interlocks
3.4.1 The condensate overflow switch is hardwired to close the cooling valve and the BAS sets a Level 3 alarm on a trip.
3.4.2 A fan status that disagrees with the command for 15 seconds sets a Level 2 fan failure alarm and closes both valves.
3.4.3 The heating valve stays closed until the fan is proven on.
