CONTRACTOR SUBMITTAL: SEQUENCE OF OPERATION (synthetic test document, case v2c02_vav_rh)
SUBMITTAL FOR: SECTION 23 09 93, VAV Terminal Units with Hot Water Reheat on AHU-2
Paragraph numbers follow the specification. Paragraphs with a letter suffix are contractor additions.

SECTION: 3.1 | Zone Temperature Setpoints
3.1.1 Each zone holds 70 °F heating and 74 °F cooling when occupied and 60 °F heating and 85 °F cooling when unoccupied.
3.1.2 Occupants may adjust the zone setpoints by at most 2 °F in either direction.

SECTION: 3.2 | Terminal Airflow Setpoints
3.2.1 Each terminal controls its discharge airflow between the scheduled minimum and the active maximum listed below.
3.2.2 VAV-201 is scheduled for 500 cfm cooling maximum, 150 cfm minimum and 250 cfm heating maximum.
3.2.3 VAV-202 is scheduled for 700 cfm cooling maximum, 210 cfm minimum and 350 cfm heating maximum.
3.2.4 VAV-203 is scheduled for 900 cfm cooling maximum, 270 cfm minimum and 450 cfm heating maximum.
3.2.5 VAV-204 is scheduled for 1100 cfm cooling maximum, 330 cfm minimum and 550 cfm heating maximum.
3.2.6 VAV-205 is scheduled for 400 cfm cooling maximum, 50 cfm minimum and 200 cfm heating maximum.
3.2.7 VAV-206 is scheduled for 600 cfm cooling maximum, 180 cfm minimum and 300 cfm heating maximum.
3.2.8 VAV-207 is scheduled for 800 cfm cooling maximum, 240 cfm minimum and 400 cfm heating maximum.
3.2.9 VAV-208 is scheduled for 1000 cfm cooling maximum, 300 cfm minimum and 500 cfm heating maximum.

SECTION: 3.3 | Damper and Valve Control
3.3.1 As the cooling loop rises from 0 to 100 percent, the airflow setpoint rises from the minimum to the maximum cooling airflow.
3.3.2 From 0 to 50 percent heating loop the discharge air temperature setpoint rises from zone temperature to 20 °F above it, and from 50 to 100 percent heating loop the airflow setpoint rises to the maximum heating airflow.
3.3.3 In cooling and in the deadband the hot water valve is closed.
3.3.4 If communication with the AHU controller is lost, each terminal controls to its minimum airflow setpoint.

SECTION: 3.4 | Terminal Alarms
3.4.1 With a setpoint above zero, airflow under 70 percent of setpoint for 5 minutes sets a Level 3 low airflow alarm, and airflow under 50 percent of setpoint for 5 minutes sets a Level 2 alarm.
3.4.2 When heating hot water is available, a discharge air temperature 15 °F under its setpoint for 10 minutes sets a Level 3 low discharge temperature alarm.
3.4.3 A damper commanded closed with measured airflow above 10 percent of the maximum cooling airflow for 10 minutes sets a Level 4 leaking damper alarm.

SECTION: 3.5 | Interlocks
3.5.1 Whenever the AHU-2 supply fan is not proven on, heating is disabled and the hot water valve is closed.
