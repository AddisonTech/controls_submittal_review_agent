SECTION 23 09 93 SEQUENCES OF OPERATION FOR HVAC CONTROLS
SYSTEM: VAV Terminal Units with Hot Water Reheat on AHU-2

PART 1 GENERAL
1.1 Provide a points schedule in tabular form for each HVAC system, with each row representing a hardware point, network point or configuration point.
1.2 Alarm generation is the monitoring of a value, comparison of the value to alarm conditions and the creation of an alarm when the conditions set for the alarm are met.
1.3 Values in this section are stated in inch-pound units. A submittal may state values in SI units when they convert to the specified value.
1.4 The sequences below are the minimum required behavior. Do not change a specified value without approval.

PART 3 EXECUTION, SEQUENCE OF OPERATION
SECTION: 3.1 | Zone Temperature Setpoints
3.1.1 Occupied zone setpoints shall be 70 °F heating and 74 °F cooling; unoccupied setpoints shall be 60 °F heating and 85 °F cooling.
3.1.2 Occupant adjustment of the zone setpoints shall be limited to plus or minus 2 °F.

SECTION: 3.2 | Terminal Airflow Setpoints
3.2.1 Each terminal shall control discharge airflow between its scheduled minimum airflow and the active maximum airflow listed below.
3.2.2 Terminal VAV-201 shall have a maximum cooling airflow of 500 cfm, a minimum airflow of 150 cfm and a maximum heating airflow of 250 cfm.
3.2.3 Terminal VAV-202 shall have a maximum cooling airflow of 700 cfm, a minimum airflow of 210 cfm and a maximum heating airflow of 350 cfm.
3.2.4 Terminal VAV-203 shall have a maximum cooling airflow of 900 cfm, a minimum airflow of 270 cfm and a maximum heating airflow of 450 cfm.
3.2.5 Terminal VAV-204 shall have a maximum cooling airflow of 1100 cfm, a minimum airflow of 330 cfm and a maximum heating airflow of 550 cfm.
3.2.6 Terminal VAV-205 shall have a maximum cooling airflow of 400 cfm, a minimum airflow of 120 cfm and a maximum heating airflow of 200 cfm.
3.2.7 Terminal VAV-206 shall have a maximum cooling airflow of 600 cfm, a minimum airflow of 180 cfm and a maximum heating airflow of 300 cfm.
3.2.8 Terminal VAV-207 shall have a maximum cooling airflow of 800 cfm, a minimum airflow of 240 cfm and a maximum heating airflow of 400 cfm.
3.2.9 Terminal VAV-208 shall have a maximum cooling airflow of 1000 cfm, a minimum airflow of 300 cfm and a maximum heating airflow of 500 cfm.

SECTION: 3.3 | Damper and Valve Control
3.3.1 In cooling, the airflow setpoint shall be reset from the minimum airflow up to the maximum cooling airflow as the cooling loop rises from 0 to 100 percent.
3.3.2 In heating, the discharge air temperature setpoint shall be reset from the zone temperature up to 20 °F above the zone temperature as the heating loop rises from 0 to 50 percent, and the airflow setpoint shall rise to the maximum heating airflow as the heating loop rises from 50 to 100 percent.
3.3.3 The hot water valve shall be closed whenever the zone is in cooling or in the deadband.
3.3.4 On loss of communication with the AHU controller, each terminal shall control to its minimum airflow setpoint.

SECTION: 3.4 | Terminal Alarms
3.4.1 If the measured airflow is below 70 percent of setpoint for 5 minutes while the setpoint is above zero, a Level 3 low airflow alarm shall be set; if it is below 50 percent of setpoint for 5 minutes, a Level 2 alarm shall be set.
3.4.2 If heating hot water is available and the discharge air temperature is 15 °F below its setpoint for 10 minutes, a Level 3 low discharge temperature alarm shall be set.
3.4.3 If the damper is commanded closed and the measured airflow stays above 10 percent of the maximum cooling airflow for 10 minutes, a Level 4 leaking damper alarm shall be set.

SECTION: 3.5 | Interlocks
3.5.1 The hot water valve shall be closed and heating disabled whenever the AHU-2 supply fan is not proven on.
