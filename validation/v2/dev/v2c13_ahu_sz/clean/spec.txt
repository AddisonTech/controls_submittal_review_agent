SECTION 23 09 93 SEQUENCES OF OPERATION FOR HVAC CONTROLS
SYSTEM: Single Zone VAV Air Handling Unit RTU-5

PART 1 GENERAL
1.1 Provide a points schedule in tabular form for each HVAC system, with each row representing a hardware point, network point or configuration point.
1.2 Alarm generation is the monitoring of a value, comparison of the value to alarm conditions and the creation of an alarm when the conditions set for the alarm are met.
1.3 Values in this section are stated in inch-pound units. A submittal may state values in SI units when they convert to the specified value.
1.4 The sequences below are the minimum required behavior. Do not change a specified value without approval.

PART 3 EXECUTION, SEQUENCE OF OPERATION
SECTION: 3.1 | Zone Setpoints and Modes
3.1.1 Occupied zone setpoints shall be 68 °F heating and 74 °F cooling; unoccupied setpoints shall be 60 °F heating and 85 °F cooling.
3.1.2 Occupant adjustment of the zone setpoints shall be limited to plus or minus 2 °F.
3.1.3 During unoccupied hours the unit shall run only in the Setback or Setup mode, as needed to hold the unoccupied setpoints.

SECTION: 3.2 | Supply Fan Control
3.2.1 The supply fan shall run continuously in the Occupied mode.
3.2.2 In the deadband the supply fan shall run at its minimum speed of 35 percent.
3.2.3 In cooling, supply fan speed shall rise from minimum to 100 percent as the cooling loop goes from 50 to 100 percent.
3.2.4 In heating, supply fan speed shall rise from minimum to 60 percent as the heating loop goes from 50 to 100 percent.

SECTION: 3.3 | Supply Air Temperature Control
3.3.1 In cooling, the supply air temperature setpoint shall be reset from 72 °F at 0 percent cooling loop down to 54 °F at 50 percent cooling loop.
3.3.2 In heating, the supply air temperature setpoint shall be reset from 72 °F at 0 percent heating loop up to 90 °F at 50 percent heating loop.
3.3.3 Mechanical cooling shall be enabled only when the outdoor air temperature is above 50 °F.

SECTION: 3.4 | Economizer
3.4.1 The economizer shall be disabled when the outdoor air temperature exceeds 70 °F and enabled again when it falls 1 °F below that limit.
3.4.2 The outdoor air damper shall be held at its minimum position whenever the unit is in heating.
3.4.3 The minimum outdoor air damper position shall be 30 percent at minimum fan speed and 18 percent at maximum fan speed, interpolated linearly between.

SECTION: 3.5 | Safeties and Interlocks
3.5.1 The duct smoke detector shall be hardwired to stop the supply fan and close the outdoor air damper, and the BAS shall set a Level 1 alarm on detection.
3.5.2 The freeze-stat shall be hardwired to stop the supply fan and shall require a manual reset.
3.5.3 A condensate overflow switch shall close the chilled water valve and set a Level 3 alarm.
3.5.4 Heating and cooling shall be disabled until the supply fan is proven on.
