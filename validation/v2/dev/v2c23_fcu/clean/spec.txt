SECTION 23 09 93 SEQUENCES OF OPERATION FOR HVAC CONTROLS
SYSTEM: Fan Coil Unit FCU-6

PART 1 GENERAL
1.1 Provide a points schedule in tabular form for each HVAC system, with each row representing a hardware point, network point or configuration point.
1.2 Alarm generation is the monitoring of a value, comparison of the value to alarm conditions and the creation of an alarm when the conditions set for the alarm are met.
1.3 Values in this section are stated in inch-pound units. A submittal may state values in SI units when they convert to the specified value.
1.4 The sequences below are the minimum required behavior. Do not change a specified value without approval.

PART 3 EXECUTION, SEQUENCE OF OPERATION
SECTION: 3.1 | Fan Control
3.1.1 The fan shall run continuously in the Occupied mode and shall cycle with demand in the Setback and Setup modes.
3.1.2 The fan shall run at its minimum speed of 20 percent in the deadband and rise to 100 percent as either loop rises from 50 to 100 percent.

SECTION: 3.2 | Supply Air Temperature Control
3.2.1 In cooling, the supply air temperature setpoint shall be reset from 72 °F at 0 percent cooling loop down to 55 °F at 50 percent cooling loop.
3.2.2 In heating, the supply air temperature setpoint shall be reset from 72 °F at 0 percent heating loop up to 95 °F at 50 percent heating loop.
3.2.3 The heating valve and the cooling valve shall never be open at the same time.

SECTION: 3.3 | Zone Setpoints
3.3.1 Occupied zone setpoints shall be 70 °F heating and 75 °F cooling; unoccupied setpoints shall be 60 °F heating and 85 °F cooling.

SECTION: 3.4 | Alarms and Interlocks
3.4.1 A condensate overflow switch shall be hardwired to close the cooling valve, and the BAS shall set a Level 3 alarm when it trips.
3.4.2 If the fan status does not match its command for 15 seconds, a Level 2 fan failure alarm shall be set and both valves shall close.
3.4.3 The heating valve shall not open until the fan is proven on.
