CONTRACTOR SUBMITTAL: SEQUENCE OF OPERATION (synthetic test document, case v2c28_chw_plant)
SUBMITTAL FOR: SECTION 23 09 93, Chilled Water Plant CHWP-5
Paragraph numbers follow the specification. Paragraphs with a letter suffix are contractor additions.

SECTION: 3.1 | Plant Enable
3.1.1 The plant enables after 5 minutes with at least 3 chiller plant requests, provided outdoor air is warmer than the 10.0 °C (50 °F) lockout.
3.1.2 The plant disables after 3 minutes with fewer than 3 requests, or once outdoor air is 1.1 °C (2 °F) below the lockout.

SECTION: 3.2 | Chiller Staging
3.2.1 A chiller is enabled only after its chilled water and condenser water isolation valves are open and evaporator and condenser flow are proven.
3.2.2 A chiller stages on when chilled water supply temperature is 1.1 °C (2 °F) above setpoint for 10 minutes with the running chillers above 85 percent load.
3.2.3 A chiller stages off after the running chillers are under 45 percent load for 15 minutes.

SECTION: 3.3 | Chilled Water Pumping
3.3.1 The lead chilled water pump runs whenever the plant is enabled.
3.3.2 Pump speed modulates to hold remote differential pressure at a setpoint that trim and respond logic resets between 41 kPa (6 psi) and 124 kPa (18 psi).
3.3.3 The minimum flow bypass valve modulates to keep chilled water flow through each running chiller above 18.9 L/s (300 gpm).

SECTION: 3.4 | Chilled Water Supply Temperature Reset
3.4.1 Trim and respond logic driven by cooling coil requests resets the chilled water supply temperature setpoint between 5.6 °C (42 °F) and 13.3 °C (56 °F).
3.4.2 When the plant enables, the chilled water supply temperature setpoint starts at 5.6 °C (42 °F).

SECTION: 3.5 | Condenser Water and Cooling Tower
3.5.1 Tower fan speed modulates to hold condenser water supply temperature at 26.7 °C (80 °F).
3.5.2 Below 18.3 °C (65 °F) condenser water supply temperature the tower fans stop and the tower bypass valve modulates open.

SECTION: 3.6 | Safeties and Interlocks
3.6.1 The BAS reads the chilled water and condenser water flow switches and disables a chiller in software when flow is lost.
3.6.2 A refrigerant monitor reading above 30 ppm starts the machine room exhaust fans and sets a Level 3 alarm.
3.6.3 The emergency stop switch at the machine room exit is hardwired to stop every chiller.
