SECTION 23 09 93 SEQUENCES OF OPERATION FOR HVAC CONTROLS
SYSTEM: Chilled Water Plant CHWP-1

PART 1 GENERAL
1.1 Provide a points schedule in tabular form for each HVAC system, with each row representing a hardware point, network point or configuration point.
1.2 Alarm generation is the monitoring of a value, comparison of the value to alarm conditions and the creation of an alarm when the conditions set for the alarm are met.
1.3 Values in this section are stated in inch-pound units. A submittal may state values in SI units when they convert to the specified value.
1.4 The sequences below are the minimum required behavior. Do not change a specified value without approval.

PART 3 EXECUTION, SEQUENCE OF OPERATION
SECTION: 3.1 | Plant Enable
3.1.1 The plant shall be enabled when there are 2 or more chiller plant requests for 5 minutes and the outdoor air temperature is above the 55 °F lockout.
3.1.2 The plant shall be disabled when there are fewer than 2 requests for 3 minutes or when the outdoor air temperature falls 2 °F below the lockout.

SECTION: 3.2 | Chiller Staging
3.2.1 Before a chiller is enabled, its chilled water and condenser water isolation valves shall be open and flow shall be proven through its evaporator and condenser.
3.2.2 The next chiller shall be staged on when the chilled water supply temperature is 2 °F above setpoint for 10 minutes and the operating chillers are above 90 percent load.
3.2.3 A chiller shall be staged off when the operating chillers are below 40 percent load for 15 minutes.

SECTION: 3.3 | Chilled Water Pumping
3.3.1 The lead chilled water pump shall run whenever the plant is enabled.
3.3.2 Pump speed shall be modulated to maintain the remote differential pressure at setpoint, reset between 5 psi and 15 psi by trim and respond logic.
3.3.3 The minimum flow bypass valve shall modulate to keep chilled water flow through each operating chiller above 240 gpm.

SECTION: 3.4 | Chilled Water Supply Temperature Reset
3.4.1 The chilled water supply temperature setpoint shall be reset between 44 °F and 54 °F by trim and respond logic driven by cooling coil requests.
3.4.2 On plant enable the chilled water supply temperature setpoint shall start at 44 °F.

SECTION: 3.5 | Condenser Water and Cooling Tower
3.5.1 Cooling tower fan speed shall be modulated to maintain the condenser water supply temperature at 85 °F.
3.5.2 The tower fans shall stop and the tower bypass valve shall modulate open when the condenser water supply temperature falls below 70 °F.

SECTION: 3.6 | Safeties and Interlocks
3.6.1 Each chiller shall be hardwired to its chilled water and condenser water flow switches so that it cannot run without proven flow.
3.6.2 When the refrigerant monitor exceeds 25 ppm, the machine room exhaust fans shall start and a Level 1 alarm shall be set.
3.6.3 The emergency stop switch at the machine room exit shall be hardwired to stop all chillers.
