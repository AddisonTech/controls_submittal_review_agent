SECTION: 3.1 | Zone Temperature Setpoints
3.1.1 Each zone holds 21.1 °C (70 °F) heating and 23.3 °C (74 °F) cooling when occupied and 15.6 °C (60 °F) heating and 29.4 °C (85 °F) cooling when unoccupied.
3.1.2 Occupants may adjust the zone setpoints by at most 1.1 °C (2 °F) in either direction.
3.1.2a Zone setpoints and the adjustment limit are adjustable at the operator workstation.

SECTION: 3.3 | Damper and Valve Control
3.3.1 As the cooling loop rises from 0 to 100 percent, the airflow setpoint rises from the minimum to the maximum cooling airflow.
3.3.2 From 0 to 50 percent heating loop the discharge air temperature setpoint rises from zone temperature to 11.1 °C (20 °F) above it, and from 50 to 100 percent heating loop the airflow setpoint rises to the maximum heating airflow.
3.3.3 In cooling and in the deadband the hot water valve is closed.
3.3.4 If communication with the AHU controller is lost, each terminal controls to its minimum airflow setpoint.
3.3.4a Each terminal controller reports its cooling and pressure reset requests to AHU-5 over the BACnet MS/TP trunk.

SECTION: 3.4 | Terminal Alarms
3.4.1 With a setpoint above zero, airflow under 70 percent of setpoint for 5 minutes sets a Level 3 low airflow alarm, and airflow under 50 percent of setpoint for 5 minutes sets a Level 2 alarm.
3.4.2 When heating hot water is available, a discharge air temperature 8.3 °C (15 °F) under its setpoint for 10 minutes sets a Level 3 low discharge temperature alarm.
