CONTRACTOR SUBMITTAL: SEQUENCE OF OPERATION (synthetic test document, case v2c12_hw_plant)
SUBMITTAL FOR: SECTION 23 09 93, Hot Water Boiler Plant HWP-2
Paragraph numbers follow the specification. Paragraphs with a letter suffix are contractor additions.

SECTION: 3.1 | Plant Enable
3.1.1 The plant enables after 5 minutes with 1 or more heating hot water plant requests while outdoor air is colder than the 15.6 °C lockout.

SECTION: 3.2 | Hot Water Supply Temperature Reset
3.2.1 The hot water supply temperature setpoint is 71.1 °C with outdoor air at -12.2 °C or colder and resets linearly to 48.9 °C with outdoor air at 18.3 °C or warmer.
3.2.2 The setpoint is limited to no lower than 48.9 °C.

SECTION: 3.3 | Boiler Staging
3.3.1 The lead boiler is enabled once the lead hot water pump is proven on and boiler flow is proven.
3.3.2 Another boiler stages on when the hot water supply temperature is 5.6 °C under setpoint for 10 minutes and the running boilers are at full fire.
3.3.3 Each boiler holds a minimum firing rate of 25 percent.
3.3.3a Boiler lead and lag order rotates weekly, adjustable at the operator workstation.

SECTION: 3.4 | Hot Water Pumping
3.4.1 Pump speed modulates to hold remote differential pressure at a setpoint reset by trim and respond logic between 34 kPa and 103 kPa.
3.4.2 If the lead pump fails, the lag pump starts and a Level 2 alarm is set.

SECTION: 3.5 | Safeties and Interlocks
3.5.1 The flow switch and low water cutoff are hardwired in each boiler firing circuit so the boiler cannot fire without proven flow and water level.
3.5.2 Each boiler high limit is set at 87.8 °C with manual reset.
3.5.3 The boiler room exit emergency shutoff switch is hardwired to stop every boiler, and the BAS sets a Level 1 alarm.
