SECTION 23 09 93 SEQUENCES OF OPERATION FOR HVAC CONTROLS
SYSTEM: Hot Water Boiler Plant HWP-2

PART 1 GENERAL
1.1 Provide a points schedule in tabular form for each HVAC system, with each row representing a hardware point, network point or configuration point.
1.2 Alarm generation is the monitoring of a value, comparison of the value to alarm conditions and the creation of an alarm when the conditions set for the alarm are met.
1.3 Values in this section are stated in inch-pound units. A submittal may state values in SI units when they convert to the specified value.
1.4 The sequences below are the minimum required behavior. Do not change a specified value without approval.

PART 3 EXECUTION, SEQUENCE OF OPERATION
SECTION: 3.1 | Plant Enable
3.1.1 The plant shall be enabled when there are 1 or more heating hot water plant requests for 5 minutes and the outdoor air temperature is below the 60 °F lockout.

SECTION: 3.2 | Hot Water Supply Temperature Reset
3.2.1 The hot water supply temperature setpoint shall be reset from 160 °F at an outdoor air temperature of 10 °F and below to 120 °F at 65 °F and above.
3.2.2 The setpoint shall never be lower than 120 °F.

SECTION: 3.3 | Boiler Staging
3.3.1 The lead boiler shall be enabled only after the lead hot water pump is proven on and boiler flow is proven.
3.3.2 The next boiler shall be staged on when the hot water supply temperature is 10 °F below setpoint for 10 minutes with the operating boilers at full fire.
3.3.3 Each boiler shall operate no lower than 25 percent firing rate.

SECTION: 3.4 | Hot Water Pumping
3.4.1 Pump speed shall be modulated to maintain the remote differential pressure at setpoint, reset between 5 psi and 15 psi by trim and respond logic.
3.4.2 On failure of the lead pump, the lag pump shall start and a Level 2 alarm shall be set.

SECTION: 3.5 | Safeties and Interlocks
3.5.1 Each boiler shall be hardwired to its flow switch and low water cutoff so that it cannot fire without proven flow and water level.
3.5.2 Each boiler high limit control shall be set at 190 °F and shall require a manual reset.
3.5.3 The emergency shutoff switch at the boiler room exit shall be hardwired to stop all boilers, and the BAS shall set a Level 1 alarm.
